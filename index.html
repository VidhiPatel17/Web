A Note From Us

We are so glad you are here. Getting married is the easy part — the joy is having the people we love in one room to celebrate it with us.

For four days our families will be busy with mehndi, haldi, garba and the wedding itself. Between all of it there will be far too much food, far too much dancing, and exactly the kind of noise we are hoping for.

Thank you for the kindness you have shown us already. We would be so happy to have you beside us as we begin this next chapter.

With all our love,
